Weekly workflow

Turn your publishing routine into a visible system. This makes the site feel active and intentional.

1. Collect

Gather sources, screenshots, links, dates, and notes in a single working folder or document.

2. Draft

Write in Google Docs, then move the final copy into a repeatable report template.

3. Publish

Export PDF, upload it to GitHub, and add a matching page summary on the site.

4. Archive

Store the source material, images, and final files in a clear folder structure for easy reuse.

Suggested repository structure

This structure keeps the website and your reports separate, organized, and easy to grow later.

my-reports-site/
├── index.html
├── about.html
├── reports.html
├── contact.html
├── assets/
│   ├── css/
│   │   └── style.css
│   ├── images/
│   └── files/
│       ├── brand-guidelines.pdf
│       └── media-kit.pdf
├── reports/
│   ├── report-one.html
│   ├── report-one.pdf
│   ├── report-two.html
│   ├── report-two.pdf
│   └── report-three.html
├── archive/
│   ├── source-notes/
│   ├── screenshots/
│   └── raw-material/
└── README.md

Where to store reports

Use /reports/ for final PDFs and report pages.

Use /archive/ for source notes, screenshots, and raw evidence.

Where to store site assets

Use /assets/css/ for styling, /assets/images/ for logos/screenshots, and /assets/files/ for extra documents.
